CRODolead · Paris · 2025

A CRO testing programme for lead generation funnels

Problem
Conversion was dropping somewhere in the lead funnels, but nobody had pinned down where or ranked the fixes.
Approach
I analysed lead volume, CPL and CVR in GA4 and Looker Studio. From that I built a ranked test roadmap, then aligned hypotheses with stakeholders and shipped the experiments.
Result
80+ A/B tests, a 20% uplift in key metrics, and optimisation playbooks the team could reuse.
